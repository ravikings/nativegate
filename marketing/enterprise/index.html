Enterprise

Make the software your organization already depends on AI-ready and microservice-ready.

nativegate is open source. Organizations can use it to evaluate and modernize existing C++ and Fortran systems without committing to a rewrite, without shipping proprietary code anywhere, and without losing the numerical equivalence they already validate against.

Enterprise services

Assessment

Modernization assessment

Map an existing codebase and identify viable modernization paths: what binds cleanly today, what needs preparation, what the interface-first plan looks like, and an honest statement of what stays Fortran.

Private

Private modernization

Run the nativegate pipeline against proprietary code inside your environment. The generator runs locally; your source never leaves your infrastructure.

Deploy

Deployment integration

Integrate the generated services into your existing infrastructure — Kubernetes layouts you already run, ingress and secrets conventions you already have, and the concurrency model the code's statefulness demands.

Golden records

Validation

Establish numerical regression baselines against your validated configurations and wire the verification into your CI, so every future change is measured against the answers you trust.

Support

Engineering support

Help teams move from a generated prototype to production: the state-and-concurrency review, capacity planning for locked Fortran services, and the operational workhorses the service doesn't include.

Everything the tool generates is free and open source. The services above exist because assessment, validation, and planning-on-real-code is work that outlives the generation itself.
